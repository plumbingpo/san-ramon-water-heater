Upfront Pricing

Honest, Upfront Pricing

Before we start any repair, we figure out exactly what's wrong. Our technicians look at the age and size of the tank, the burner or elements, the relief valve, the venting, and any signs of corrosion or seepage. Then you get an honest recommendation and the full price up front, whether the job is swapping a thermocouple or replacing the whole unit. No hidden fees, no surprise add-ons.

A lot of water heater failures give quiet warnings long before the tank quits. Keep an eye out for discolored or rusty hot water, a rumbling tank, hot water that smells like rotten eggs, or showers that go cold sooner than they used to. Behind those symptoms are usually a thick layer of sediment, a spent anode rod, or a worn dip tube. An early service call at the first sign usually costs far less than mopping up after a ruptured tank.

We also help homeowners plan ahead. Most tank water heaters last 8 to 12 years, and a planned water heater replacement is nearly always less expensive and less stressful than an emergency swap with water on the floor. Our technicians know how local water conditions wear on tanks over time, so we can tell you honestly whether your heater has years left or is living on borrowed time.

Annual Flushing

Water Heater Maintenance That Adds Years

A maintenance visit from San Ramon Water Heater is far more than opening the drain valve. Our technician flushes the sediment, pulls and inspects the anode rod, lifts and tests the relief valve, inspects the burner, flue, and draft on gas models, tests the elements and thermostats on electric units, and confirms a safe water temperature. Minor faults are caught while they're still cheap to fix, and your water heater stays running clean and efficient.

Most homes do best with one flush a year, and homes with hard water may need a visit every six months. We adjust the schedule to the age of your heater, how hard your household works it, and the mineral content of your water, which controls how quickly sediment builds up and how long an anode rod lasts.

The savings show up quickly. A flushed tank heats water with less effort, a fresh anode rod can add years to the tank, and a tested relief valve means the tank can safely vent pressure if something goes wrong. Compared with the cost of an early replacement, a maintenance plan is money well spent.

Right-Sized Hot Water

The Right Size Water Heater for Your Home

An undersized water heater runs out halfway through the second shower, while an oversized one burns fuel keeping gallons you never use hot around the clock. San Ramon Water Heater sizes every replacement and new installation to your actual household. For tank models, that means looking at the first-hour rating to your morning rush, not just copying the gallon size on the old tank. For tankless models, it means sizing the gallons per minute against the fixtures you run at once and how cold your incoming water gets in winter.

Households change over time. A growing family or an added bathroom can overwhelm the tank that used to be plenty. A tankless unit heats water on demand and never runs out as long as you stay within the rated flow, while heat pump water heaters draw far less electricity than a standard electric tank. Whether you're comparing tank and tankless, or gas and electric, we'll explain the real trade-offs in upfront cost, operating cost, venting and gas line needs, and installation work.

Frequent Failures

The Water Heater Repairs Local Homes Need Most

Homes in San Ramon run into a familiar set of water heater failures, and each one calls for a different fix. San Ramon Water Heater handles them all: no hot water, water that never gets hot enough, pilots that won't stay lit, electric tanks with burned-out elements, rumbling tanks full of sediment, rusty or smelly water, leaking relief valves, and tankless units flashing error codes. Our technicians identify the exact fault and target the repair to the component that actually failed.

Not every water heater problem is equally urgent. A bad element only cuts your hot water supply. A relief valve spitting water, on the other hand, is a safety device doing its job and telling you something is wrong, and water pooling under the tank can quietly ruin subfloors and drywall before anyone notices. Smell gas? Leave the house and call your gas utility before anything else. Our technicians carry the parts to swap elements, thermostats, thermocouples, gas valves, and relief valves, add a thermal expansion tank when pressure is the culprit, and make leak repairs at fittings and drain valves.
