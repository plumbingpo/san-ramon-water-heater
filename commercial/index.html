Code & Inspections

Code-Compliant Installation and Documentation

Badly installed or neglected hot water equipment can lead to carbon monoxide hazards, scalding, Legionella risk in lukewarm storage, and expensive water damage when a tank fails. That's a real liability for a commercial property owner with employees, customers, and tenants in the building. Our commercial installations and inspections cover venting, combustion air, gas line sizing, relief valve discharge, expansion tanks, mixing valves, and code items like drain pans and seismic straps where required, followed by a written report that ranks each risk by priority and recommends specific fixes.

Good records matter almost as much as good repairs. We keep a documentation binder with detailed service logs, temperature readings, and relief valve test dates, so you can demonstrate due diligence to landlords, insurers, and inspectors. Equipment that works hardest, in kitchens, laundries, and central plant rooms, is put on a more frequent service schedule.

Emergency Service

After-Hours Commercial Water Heater Response

When a commercial water heater fails, the clock starts immediately. Dish rooms stall, salon chairs empty, guests call the front desk, and tenants start complaining. Calls to our commercial line get an experienced technician dispatched fast for emergency repairs anywhere in San Ramon and the surrounding area, and planned work can be booked after hours to limit downtime.

Failed gas valves, burned-out elements, ignition faults, tripped high-limit switches, leaking tanks, stalled recirculation pumps, tankless lockouts: our technicians handle all of it on tank, tankless, and boiler-fed systems. Our first priority is diagnosing the fault and getting hot water back as quickly as possible, and in a multi-heater system we isolate the failed unit so the others carry the load. Once it's running, we set up a maintenance schedule so it doesn't happen again, and each response is fully documented for your records.
